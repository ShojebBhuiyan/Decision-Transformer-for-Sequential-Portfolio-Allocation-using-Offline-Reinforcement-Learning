\begin{abstract}
We frame multi-asset portfolio allocation as an offline sequence-modeling problem using a Decision Transformer (DT). Historical market trajectories synthesized from diverse behavior policies (mean-variance, momentum, equal-weight, noisy rollouts) train a causal transformer to predict portfolio weights conditioned on Return-to-Go (RTG). We evaluate on 30 years of daily close prices (1995--2025) across a 14-asset Universe~A with cash and synthetic bond exposure, benchmarking against classical strategies, Behavior Cloning, and offline RL methods (TD3+BC, IQL, CQL).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Online reinforcement learning in financial markets is impractical due to transaction costs, non-stationarity, and the inability to replay history. Offline RL and sequence modeling offer an alternative: learn from fixed historical trajectories without environment interaction. The Decision Transformer~\cite{chen2021dt} reformulates RL as autoregressive modeling over $(\hat{R}_t, s_t, a_t)$ tuples, enabling conditioning on desired future returns at inference.

\section{Problem Formulation}
We define a Markov Decision Process over $N=14$ investable assets plus cash:
\begin{align}
    s_t &= (w'_{t-1}, X_t, I_t), \quad a_t \in \Delta^N, \\
    R_t &= \ln\!\left(a_t^\top y_t - \mu \sum_i |w_{i,t} - w'_{i,t-1}|\right),
\end{align}
where $y_t$ is the relative price return vector, $\mu=0.001$ is the proportional transaction cost, and $\hat{R}_t = \sum_{t'=t}^{T} R_{t'}$ is the undiscounted Return-to-Go.

\section{Data}
We use the 30-year Stock Market Dataset (1995--2025) with daily closing prices for indices, equities, commodities, currencies, and bonds. After aligning to NYSE trading days and constructing Universe~A (2000-09-01 onward), we obtain 6,369 trading days. Non-investable series (VIX, WTI, term spreads) serve as state features only.

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Trajectory Synthesis}
Behavior policies generate 540 episodes of length $T=252$ from the training window (2000--2016): MVO (Ledoit-Wolf), min-variance, risk parity, momentum, equal-weight, buy-and-hold, plus Dirichlet-noisy variants.

\subsection{Decision Transformer}
A causal GPT with $K=30$ context, $d_{\text{model}}=128$, 3 layers, and 4 heads embeds interleaved RTG/state/action tokens. A softmax head enforces simplex constraints. Training minimizes MSE on action predictions.

\subsection{Baselines}
Classical: buy-and-hold, equal-weight, MVO, min-variance, risk parity, momentum. Imitation: transformer BC (no RTG). Offline RL: TD3+BC, IQL, CQL. Online RL (reference): PPO, SAC, A2C.

\section{Experiments}
\textbf{Splits:} Train 2000--2016, validation 2017--2019, test 2020--2025. Walk-forward folds cover six 2-year blocks.

\textbf{Metrics:} CAGR, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, maximum drawdown, turnover, VaR/CVaR.

The Decision Transformer trains successfully (val MSE $\approx 0.009$) on minimal feature states (returns + macro + events, lookback 10). Full GPU training with complete feature sets and 5 seeds is recommended for publication-quality DT vs.\ baseline comparisons.

\section{Discussion}
\textbf{Limitations:} Close-only data precludes volume-based indicators. CPU training used reduced epochs and subsampled trajectories. WTI negative prices (2020-04-20) require feature-only treatment. Online RL baselines use environment interaction and are not directly comparable to offline methods.

\textbf{Future work:} CUDA training with full feature set, RTG calibration curves, Universe~B crypto robustness, and statistical significance testing (block-bootstrap, deflated Sharpe).
